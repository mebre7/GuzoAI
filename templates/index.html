Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How accurate are the flight prices?

+

Our AI agents fetch real-time data from aviation databases, ensuring prices are accurate within minutes. We always recommend checking the final price on the booking site.

Can I customize the itinerary after it's generated?

+

Absolutely! The itinerary is a starting point. You can modify activities, swap hotels, adjust dates, and make any changes you like. You can also re-run the planner with new preferences.

Does GuzoAI book my flights and hotels?

+

GuzoAI creates the perfect itinerary for you, but you handle the bookings directly through the travel providers. This ensures you get the best rates and your own booking confirmation.

Is my travel data private and secure?

+

Yes! All your travel data is encrypted and stored securely. We follow industry-standard security practices and never share your information with third parties.

What makes GuzoAI different from other travel planners?

+

GuzoAI uses multi-agent AI technology where different specialized agents work together - one finds flights, another handles hotels, another curates activities. This collaborative approach ensures better recommendations than traditional tools.

Can I get support if I need help?

+

Of course! We offer email support on all plans, and phone support for Professional and Enterprise plans. Our support team is available 24/7 to help.
